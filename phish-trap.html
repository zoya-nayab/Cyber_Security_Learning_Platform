⚠️ You clicked a phishing link!

You've Been Phished!

Don't worry — this is a safe simulation. But in real life, clicking this link could have compromised your account, installed malware, or stolen your credentials.

🔍 What gave it away?

1
Fake sender domain — The email came from security@paypa1-alerts.com not paypal.com. Notice the "1" instead of "l".
2
Urgency tactic — "Verify within 24 hours or your account will be permanently suspended" is designed to make you panic and act without thinking.
3
Generic greeting — Real companies personalize emails with your name. "Dear Customer" is a red flag.
4
Suspicious link — Hovering over the link would have shown it doesn't go to paypal.com. Always check before clicking!

✅ What to do when you spot a phishing email

  • Do NOT click any links or download attachments
  • Report it to your email provider as spam/phishing
  • If you accidentally clicked, change your passwords immediately
  • Enable 2FA on all important accounts
  • Notify your IT/security team if it happened at work
